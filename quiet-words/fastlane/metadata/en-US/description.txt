Quiet Words is word search for grown-ups: calm, themed and honest. No ads. No purchases. No data collected. Just you and the letters.

HOW TO PLAY
Find the theme's words hidden in the grid. Drag from the first letter to the last, or tap the first letter and then the last. Words run across, down and diagonally, and backwards on Hard. A short interactive tutorial shows you how.

A HIDDEN WORD IN EVERY PUZZLE
Every letter on the board belongs to a word, except a few: read in order, the leftover letters spell one more word of the theme. Guess it early for a bonus, or watch it appear when you find the last word.

THEMES WITH SOMETHING TO SAY
Eleven themes written by hand, each with around two hundred words: Ocean, Space, Botany, Biology, Mountain, Coffee & Tea, Food, Geography, Music, Renaissance & Industry and 20th Century & Today. Familiar words and words with a craft, from all over the world.

ENGLISH OR SPANISH WORDS
Choose the language of the words, separate from the app's, and switch anytime with one tap. Each language has its own lists, its own album and its own daily leaderboard.

A NEW CHALLENGE EVERY DAY
The same board for everyone, in each language. Gentle on Monday, hard on the weekend. Play it as often as you like, in one language or both, keep your streak going and revisit any of the last 30 days.

YOUR ALBUM
Every word you find goes into the album of its theme. Complete a theme to earn its badge, and look up any word you've found in your device's dictionary.

REWARDS YOU EARN, NEVER BUY
Achievements unlock markers, board letters, color themes and an alternate app icon. Everything is earned by playing.

GAME CENTER, IF YOU WANT IT
Daily leaderboards in English and Spanish, best scores, streaks and 10 achievements, with friends and the world. Your progress follows you across your devices through your own iCloud. Prefer to stay signed out? Everything still works, offline too.

RESPECTFUL BY DESIGN
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions.
• No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
• Your games and your album stay on your device.

IPHONE, IPAD AND MAC
On iPhone, iPad and Mac. Supports Dark Mode, Dynamic Type for the word lists, VoiceOver letter by letter and Reduce Motion, with larger letters and a high-contrast theme always available.
